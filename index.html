Estate Sale Catalog

From Auntie's House

A cataloged collection of household treasures — each item researched, valued, and matched to the best place to sell it.

Add an item

Upload one or more photos (first photo is the cover), let the AI scan fill in the details, then review and save.

Sale settings

Contact details power the "I'm interested" button on every item; the rest appears in the Sale Terms box for buyers. Leave any field blank to hide it. Never put the house address here.

Back up & publish

Changes save automatically in this browser. To put them on your live site, download the data file and replace items-data.js in your site folder, then re-upload the folder to your host.

AI scan settings

The scan uses Claude to identify the item and suggest details. Paste your Anthropic API key — it stays only in this browser and is never included in the exported site files. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

Values are realistic resale estimates, not appraisals. Catalog maintained by LaFaries Mortimer.
